# 📚 Books

Fifteen detailed book summaries, 234 Paul Graham essays, and thirteen country
and regional proverb collections about classic epics, military strategy, economics, company building, product-led growth,
technology, design, writing, careers, culture, and the people who shaped
consequential organizations. Read straight through for a broad study of
strategy and leadership, or choose a subject below. Each summary preserves the
book's reasoning, examples, tensions, and caveats rather than reducing it to a
list of slogans.

**~71 hr 20 min total** · fifteen book summaries, 234 essays, and thirteen proverb collections across twelve subjects

## IKEA · ~1 hr 7 min

1. [Design by IKEA: A Cultural History](/courses/books/ikea/design-by-ikea) — ~43 min
2. [The Testament of a Furniture Dealer](/courses/books/ikea/the-testament-of-a-furniture-dealer) — ~24 min

Read these together for two perspectives: Sara Kristoffersson examines how IKEA
constructed and circulated its identity, while Ingvar Kamprad states the
founder's operating philosophy in his own prescriptive terms.

## Reid Hoffman · ~2 hr 1 min

1. [Blitzscaling](/courses/books/reid-hoffman/blitzscaling) — ~41 min
2. [The Start-up of You](/courses/books/reid-hoffman/the-start-up-of-you) — ~41 min
3. [The Alliance](/courses/books/reid-hoffman/the-alliance) — ~39 min

These summaries move from scaling companies to adapting individual careers and
then to rebuilding trust between employers and employees.

## Steve Jobs · ~40 min

1. [Return to the Little Kingdom](/courses/books/steve-jobs/return-to-the-little-kingdom) — ~40 min

Michael Moritz follows Apple from the histories of Jobs and Wozniak through the
garage years, rapid growth, professionalization, conflict, decline, and Jobs's
eventual return.

## Elon Musk · ~43 min

1. [Elon Musk](/courses/books/elon-musk/elon-musk) — ~43 min

Ashlee Vance traces Musk's childhood and early companies through the formative
years of SpaceX, Tesla, and SolarCity, including the human costs and contested
claims surrounding that ambition.

## Founders and Founding Teams · ~2 hr 56 min

1. [Founders at Work](/courses/books/founders-at-work/founders-at-work) — ~1 hr 47 min
2. [The Founder's Dilemmas](/courses/books/founders-and-founding-teams/the-founders-dilemmas) — ~1 hr 9 min

Jessica Livingston's interviews reconstruct the uncertain beginnings of PayPal,
Hotmail, Apple, Gmail, Flickr, and many other startups, emphasizing pivots,
craftsmanship, financing, persistence, and the operating reality hidden by later
success. Noam Wasserman complements those firsthand accounts with research on
career timing, cofounder selection, roles, equity, hiring, investors, succession,
and the recurring tradeoff between founder wealth and control.

## Product-Led Growth · ~36 min

1. [Product-Led Growth](/courses/books/product-led-growth/product-led-growth) — ~36 min

Wes Bush explains how to choose a free-trial, freemium, demo, or hybrid model;
align pricing and onboarding around customer outcomes; and improve expansion and
retention without assuming that free access automatically creates value.

## Paul Graham Essays · ~48 hr 17 min

[Browse all 234 essays](/courses/books/paul-graham), ordered from the newest to
the earliest. The collection spans startups, technology, programming languages,
writing, ambition, work, and independent thought. Each page links back to its
original source.

## Adam Smith · ~55 min

1. [The Wealth of Nations](/courses/books/adam-smith/the-wealth-of-nations) — ~55 min

Adam Smith follows the sources of national wealth through specialization,
wages, profits, rent, capital, trade, colonial monopoly, public institutions,
taxation, and debt. The summary covers all five books and all 32 chapters,
including the longer internal parts and appendices, while distinguishing his
historical claims from modern conclusions.

1. [China](/courses/books/proverbs/china) — ~8 min
2. [Korea](/courses/books/proverbs/korea) — ~7 min
3. [Japan](/courses/books/proverbs/japan) — ~6 min
4. [The Netherlands](/courses/books/proverbs/netherlands) — ~6 min
5. [Germany](/courses/books/proverbs/germany) — ~5 min
6. [Sweden](/courses/books/proverbs/sweden) — ~4 min
7. [Norway](/courses/books/proverbs/norway) — ~4 min
8. [Denmark](/courses/books/proverbs/denmark) — ~4 min
9. [Switzerland](/courses/books/proverbs/switzerland) — ~6 min
10. [Austria](/courses/books/proverbs/austria) — ~5 min
11. [India](/courses/books/proverbs/india) — ~6 min
12. [Britain](/courses/books/proverbs/britain) — ~5 min

Proverbs and everyday sayings grouped by theme, each with the original wording,
romanization where needed, a literal translation, its usual meaning, and the
cultural habit it reflects. Read them side by side to compare how cultures
talk about modesty, effort, harmony, thrift, and home, and where the same image
travels across borders. The collections are curated, not exhaustive, and
proverbs describe shared ideals rather than how every person behaves.
Switzerland's page includes Swiss German and shared French and Italian sayings;
India's initial selection identifies its Hindi and Tamil sources rather than
claiming to cover every language.
Britain's page brings together English, Scots, and Welsh examples, with
Great Britain distinguished from the wider United Kingdom.

## Homer · ~7 hr 31 min

1. [The Iliad](/courses/books/homer/the-iliad) — ~3 hr 16 min
2. [The Odyssey](/courses/books/homer/the-odyssey) — ~4 hr 15 min

The two oldest epics of Greek literature, retold book by book in clear, simple
English for readers whose first language is not English. The Iliad follows the
anger of Achilles, the death of Patroclus and Hector, and Priam's visit to ask
for his son's body. The Odyssey follows Telemachus's search for his father,
Odysseus's adventures at sea, and his return to Ithaca to face the suitors.
Each summary covers all 24 books and uses the Greek names of gods and heroes.

## Virgil · ~3 hr 39 min

1. [The Aeneid](/courses/books/virgil/the-aeneid) — ~3 hr 39 min

The Roman epic that continues Homer's story from the Trojan side, retold book
by book in clear, simple English. Aeneas escapes burning Troy, loves and leaves
Dido in Carthage, visits his father in the underworld, and fights Turnus for a
new home in Italy. The summary covers all 12 books, uses Virgil's Roman names
for the gods, and separates the story from later interpretation.

## Sun Tzu · ~1 hr 44 min

1. [The Art of War](/courses/books/sun-tzu/the-art-of-war) — ~1 hr 44 min

Sun Tzu's ancient Chinese guide to strategy, explained chapter by chapter in
clear, simple English. The summary covers all 13 chapters, from planning and
deception to terrain, the nine situations, fire, and spies. It keeps Sun Tzu's
own teaching separate from the examples added by Lionel Giles and the Chinese
commentators, and marks modern business readings as later uses.

## Reading method

Use the summaries to recover an argument, compare authors, or decide which
original work deserves a deeper read; summaries do not replace the original books.
Reading times use approximately 200 prose words per minute; the total adds the
book estimates and the estimate for the full essay collection. The Paul Graham pages preserve the essay
text and identify the original source. Claims and judgments belong to the named
authors unless a page explicitly marks a qualification or counterpoint.
