# Supplement Stack Ranked by Cognitive & High-Performance Impact

The supplement aisle is where “maybe a little useful” puts on a lab coat and
starts charging premium prices. Treat this page as a decision reference, not a
shopping list.

<Meme top="sleep: inconsistent · meals: chaos · movement: none" visual="💊💊💊" bottom="obviously the missing variable is capsule number eight" />

:::caution General information only
This ranking is educational and is not a personalized recommendation to use any
supplement or stack. Evidence, product formulations, and individual risks vary.
Review medications and health conditions with a pharmacist or clinician, and
verify the current Canadian product label before use.
:::

## Check the Foundation First

<SupplementGate />

## Magnesium (L-Threonate / Bisglycinate) — **10 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **Foundational Core**
* **Why it ranks #1:** Magnesium supports normal nervous-system function, sleep, and stress regulation, with the greatest potential benefit when dietary intake is inadequate. Evidence that L-Threonate improves cognition in healthy adults is still limited.
* **When and how to take it:** Take in the evening, with or without food, following the product label's **elemental magnesium** amount. Food may reduce stomach upset. Do not combine multiple magnesium products without calculating the total supplemental amount. Separate magnesium by at least 2–4 hours from levothyroxine, tetracycline or quinolone antibiotics, and bisphosphonates. Ask a clinician before use if you have kidney disease.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [AOR Magnesium L-Threonate, 120 Vegan Capsules](https://www.amazon.ca/AOR-Magnesium-L-Threonate-Vegan-Capsules/dp/B0HBLDMPYX)

## Creatine Monohydrate — **9 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **High-Efficacy Bioenergetic**
* **Why it ranks #2:** Creatine helps replenish ATP in muscle and brain tissue. Research strongly supports its physical-performance benefits and suggests cognitive benefits may be greatest during sleep deprivation, high mental demand, or low dietary creatine intake.
* **When and how to take it:** Take **3–5 g once daily** at any consistent time, mixed thoroughly into water or another drink. Taking it with a meal can reduce stomach discomfort. A loading phase is optional and unnecessary for routine use. Stay normally hydrated and ask a clinician before use if you have kidney disease or are being monitored for kidney function; creatine can raise measured creatinine without necessarily indicating kidney damage.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [PVL Creapure Creatine Monohydrate, Unflavoured, 410 g](https://www.amazon.ca/PVL-Monohydrate-Canadian-Owned-Informed-Choice-Unflavoured/dp/B0C5GDDZB9)

## L-Theanine — **8 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **Immediate Acute Regulator**
* **Why it ranks #3:** L-Theanine can promote calm attention without strong sedation. Its most consistent practical use is alongside caffeine, where it may reduce subjective tension and improve some measures of attention.
* **When and how to take it:** Take **100–200 mg about 30–60 minutes before** demanding work, either alone or with a modest caffeine dose. Start at the low end to assess drowsiness or dizziness. Avoid combining it with sedatives without professional guidance, and monitor for light-headedness if you have low blood pressure or use blood-pressure medication.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [Natural Factors Stress-Relax L-Theanine, 60 Vegetarian Capsules](https://www.amazon.ca/Natural-Factors-Stress-Relax-Suntheanine-L-Theanine/dp/B0009MR3GS)

## Choline Sources (CDP-Choline / Alpha-GPC) — **7 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **Targeted Nootropic Support**
* **Why it ranks #4:** Choline is required to synthesize acetylcholine and supports normal neurological function. CDP-Choline and Alpha-GPC may be useful in specific circumstances, but evidence that they consistently enhance cognition in healthy, adequately nourished adults is mixed.
* **When and how to take it:** Choose **one choline form**, follow its label, and take it in the morning or early afternoon, preferably with food. Start with the lowest labeled serving rather than stacking forms. Stop and reassess if it causes headache, nausea, sweating, low blood pressure, or a fishy body odour. Ask a clinician before combining it with cholinergic or anticholinergic medication.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [Natural Factors Phosphatidyl Choline, 90 Softgels](https://www.amazon.ca/NATURAL-FACTORS-Phosphatidyl-Choline-1200Mg/dp/B0BLTZ95SC)

> **Product note:** The linked product contains phosphatidylcholine, not Alpha-GPC or CDP-Choline. These forms are not dose-equivalent.

## Rhodiola Rosea — **6 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **Acute Stress & Fatigue Buffer**
* **Why it ranks #5:** Rhodiola may modestly reduce perceived fatigue and stress during demanding periods. Results vary because extracts differ in rosavin and salidroside content, and evidence for broad cognitive enhancement remains limited.
* **When and how to take it:** Follow the standardized extract's label and take it in the **morning**, ideally 30 minutes before breakfast or with food if it irritates your stomach. Avoid taking it late in the day because it can feel stimulating. Do not use it without medical guidance if you have bipolar disorder, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take antidepressants or other serotonergic medication.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [AOR Rhodiola Rosea, 170 mg, 60 Capsules](https://www.amazon.ca/AOR-Rhodiola-Capsules-Helps-Stress/dp/B07PPJKCVP)

## NAD+ Precursors (NMN / NR) — **4 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **Experimental Metabolic Support**
* **Why it ranks #6:** NMN and NR can increase NAD-related metabolites, but evidence that they meaningfully improve cognition, energy, jet lag, or work performance in healthy adults is not yet strong. They also carry a relatively high cost.
* **When and how to take it:** Choose **NMN or NR, not both**, and follow the product label. Morning use with food is a practical default if it causes nausea on an empty stomach. Long-term safety data remain incomplete. Seek medical guidance before use during pregnancy or breastfeeding, during cancer treatment, or with a history of cancer.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [AOR NMN 600 mg, 60 Capsules](https://www.amazon.ca/AOR-Nicotinamide-Mononucleotide-Supplement-Vitality/dp/B0GGJPTXTS)

## Acetyl-L-Carnitine (ALCAR) — **3 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **Conditional Neuro-Metabolic Support**
* **Why it ranks #7:** ALCAR participates in fatty-acid metabolism and can cross the blood-brain barrier. Some evidence supports its use in particular clinical or age-related contexts, but noticeable cognitive benefits in healthy adults are inconsistent.
* **When and how to take it:** Follow the product label and take it in the **morning or early afternoon**, with food if needed for nausea. Avoid late-day use if it disrupts sleep. Ask a clinician before use if you have a seizure disorder, hypothyroidism, or take warfarin; do not automatically combine it with a choline supplement.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [AOR ALCAR, 500 mg, 60 Capsules](https://www.amazon.ca/AOR-ALCAR-Capsules-Carnitine-Brain/dp/B00FWC4L92)

## CoQ10 / Ubiquinol — **1 / 10**

* **Impact Level:** **Conditional Cardiovascular & Mitochondrial Support**
* **Why it ranks #8:** CoQ10 is essential to mitochondrial energy production and may be useful for people with specific deficiencies, medical conditions, or medication-related depletion. Evidence for a noticeable improvement in focus or working memory in healthy adults is weak.
* **When and how to take it:** Take the labeled serving with a **fat-containing breakfast or lunch** to improve absorption. Avoid taking it near bedtime if it feels activating. CoQ10 can reduce warfarin's anticoagulant effect and may add to blood-pressure lowering, so discuss it with a clinician if either applies.
* **How to buy in Canada:** [CanPrev Ubiquinol 100 mg, 60 Softgels](https://www.amazon.ca/CanPrev-Ubiquinol-100mg-60-Softgels/dp/B01HH19EBA)

## Using the Stack Safely

* Add only **one supplement at a time** and keep the rest of the routine stable for at least one to two weeks so benefits and adverse effects can be attributed correctly.
* Do not start all eight as a single stack. Several have limited evidence for healthy adults, and combining them increases cost and interaction risk without establishing which one helps.
* Prefer products with independent quality testing and verify the medicinal ingredient, amount per serving, and expiry date on the current label; Amazon listings can change.
* Check with a pharmacist or clinician before use if you take prescription medication, have a chronic condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are preparing for surgery.
* Stop and seek medical advice for severe or persistent symptoms. Supplements do not substitute for adequate sleep, nutrition, exercise, or treatment of an underlying medical condition.

## Three Things to Keep

- A ranking is not an individualized recommendation or a reason to stack items.
- Stabilize the baseline, add one variable, and define what you will measure.
- Medication, condition, pregnancy, surgery, and interaction questions belong
	with a pharmacist or clinician.
