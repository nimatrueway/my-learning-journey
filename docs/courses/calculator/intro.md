# 🧮 Calculator

Numbers are useful when the formula and its limits stay visible. This course is
a home for compact calculators that show their work instead of turning one input
into a mysterious verdict.

## Modules

1. [BMI & Baby Growth Calculator](/courses/calculator/body-measures/bmi) — ~7 min

:::note Educational scope
These calculators provide general educational information, not diagnosis or
individualized medical advice. Discuss health concerns with a qualified clinician.
:::
