# Courses

Five courses about building systems, understanding people, and turning ideas
into practice. Explore a subject through short lessons, worked examples, and
exercises, with interactive visualizations or hands-on labs where they help
make the ideas concrete.

## [Deep Learning, No Snoozing](/courses/deep-learning/)

Follow a neural network from its first neuron to transformers, language models,
and audio and video models. Interactive visualizations make forward passes,
gradients, attention, and training behavior tangible; small projects turn the
diagrams into working models. The recurring cat supplies the questionable
predictions. You learn how to correct them.

## [Psychology](/courses/performance-foundations/)

Practical ways to work with attention, fear, confidence, anger, and uncertainty.
Lessons connect familiar situations at work and in relationships to small
exercises: testing a worrying prediction, handling criticism, setting online
boundaries, or turning endless learning into useful action.

## [Startup School](/courses/startup-school/)

Explore the decisions behind starting and running a company: choosing
cofounders, understanding customers, finding growth, raising capital, and
handling setbacks. Lessons draw on Dalton and Michael's startup talks, explain
the reasoning behind the advice, and end with a bounded exercise for applying it.

## [Apache Lucene & Search](/courses/lucene-search/)

Build an understanding of full-text, vector, and hybrid search with Apache
Lucene and Java. Follow documents through indexing, text analysis, ranking,
filtering, and retrieval, then investigate relevance and performance with
runnable labs. Learn how to tell whether a search change actually helps.

## [RDBMS, MVCC & PostgreSQL Internals](/courses/postgres-internals/)

Trace a database query from relational constraints and transactions to snapshots,
heap pages, indexes, and execution plans. PostgreSQL labs expose how concurrency,
WAL, vacuum, replication, and recovery work beneath SQL, so performance and
correctness become things you can investigate rather than guess at.
