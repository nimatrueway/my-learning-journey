# James Clear

## About this collection

The Power of Imperfect Starts is the title of this curated reading page. It is not a
verified table of contents for a Persian book or a claim that James Clear
published an English book under this title. The exact connection between these
articles and a particular Persian edition remains unconfirmed without that
edition's table of contents and publication details.

Each card opens the full article from the supplied HTML, with a short editorial
summary at the top and a link to James Clear's source page. Article text,
illustrations, and footnotes retain their original attribution. Website menus,
newsletter forms, and other surrounding site elements are not included.

Times on the cards and sidebar estimate reading the article and its summary at
200 words per minute. The thirteen entries total **~1 hr 34 min**.

### In Books

- [Good and Bad Procrastination, by Paul Graham](/courses/books/paul-graham/procrastination) —

  includes imperfect early releases and learning from real use, in the more

### In Psychology

- [From Learning to Doing](/courses/performance-foundations/part-9-from-learning-to-doing) —
  the closest match to motion versus action, including a smaller useful solution

- [Acting Despite Fear](/courses/performance-foundations/part-2-acting-despite-fear) —

  beginning without certainty and planning how to recover when an attempt fails.
